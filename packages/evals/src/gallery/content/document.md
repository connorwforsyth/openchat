# Northcote shop: opening plan

Copperloaf's fourth shop opens at **212 High Street, Northcote** on Saturday 6 February 2027. This plan covers the fit-out, hiring, the opening menu and how we'll know it's working. It's owned by Mia Tran (operations) and reviewed every Monday at the Fitzroy production meeting.

## Goals for the first quarter

1. Cover the shop's weekly running costs by week 10.
2. Sell 420 loaves and 900 pastries a week by the end of March.
3. Keep our Google rating at 4.7 or better while the new team settles in.
4. Move at least a fifth of Northcote's bread to pre-orders through the app.

## Fit-out

The site was a florist, so the floor and plumbing need the most work. We're keeping the street-facing window and the pressed-metal ceiling, and the counter sits on the left so the queue doesn't block the door on Saturdays.

- **Bake floor.** One four-deck Tagliavini oven, a 60-litre spiral mixer and a retarder for 240 loaves. Dough for Northcote is still mixed at Fitzroy for the first three months.
- **Front of house.** A 4.2-metre counter, a two-group espresso machine and seating for 14 inside and 10 on the footpath.
- **Pickup shelf.** Pre-orders wait on a labelled shelf by the door, so app customers never queue.

## Opening menu

We open with a short menu and add to it once the bake floor runs smoothly. Bread is the anchor; pastries and coffee bring people in every day.

## Risks and what we'll do

> The oven is the one thing we can't open without. Everything else has a workaround.

- **Oven arrives late.** It ships from Italy in December. If it slips past 18 January, we open with bread baked at Fitzroy and delivered at 6 am, and move the public opening back one week at most.
- **Can't hire the 4 am bakers.** Tomás and one Fitzroy baker cover the first four weeks on a rotating roster.
- **Slow first month.** We budget for a loss in February and look at the numbers again at week 6, not before.

## How we'll measure it

Each Monday we look at loaves and pastries sold, waste as a share of what we baked, average wait at the counter on Saturday morning, and the share of bread sold by pre-order. The numbers live in the [Northcote dashboard](https://copperloaf.com.au/team/northcote) and the fit-out budget spreadsheet.
